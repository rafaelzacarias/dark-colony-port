## Cause

the pre-advance simulation snapshot at tick **1135**. The original opening
`reinforce2 1 30 27 69 1 ...` has already delivered actor `transport:193:0`,
simulation ID 45, type 69, team 1. It receives:

```json
{"type":"move","unitIds":[45],"target":{"x":7,"y":70}}
```

Its persisted `issuedTick` is 1135. At tick 1161 it remains on that order and
has moved from `(30,27)` to `(30,32)`. The second decision correctly suppresses
a duplicate. Requiring a new order dated 1136 or later was incorrect.

The census also contains team-1 static type-41 guns and type-86 objects; neither
is the commanded actor. The two type-0 soldiers requested by the activation
trigger are not yet present in this tick-1161 observed census. ALIEN02's TRO
has no aimsg action. This failure is not evidence of a weight-gating defect.
